# Resident art prompt set

The production portraits were generated with the built-in image-generation model from the settled
Hearth character briefs.

## Shared direction

Create a square editorial character portrait for Hearth, a warm modern Windows working-home app.
Use restrained near-cinematic realism with gentle illustrative polish, warm practical light, rich
natural texture, and a calm contemporary interior. Keep the subject expressive and approachable,
not glossy, corporate, fantasy-costumed, or cartoonish. Compose the head and shoulders clearly for
a small rounded-square interface crop. No text, logos, border, frame, or watermark.

## Maker

A man in his late thirties to early forties: intelligent, capable, slightly scruffy, and easy to
talk to, with the relaxed edge of a tech nerd, metalhead, and occasional stoner without becoming a
caricature. Dark casual layers, subtle alternative details, dry amused expression, and a warm
workshop-study background with softly blurred tools, electronics, wood, and amber light. He should
look like someone who can solve a hard technical problem while talking naturally.

## Librarian

A woman in her mid-to-late thirties: cute but not twee, intelligent, warm, grounded, and easy to
talk to, with discernment visible in her eyes. Give her a slightly alternative modern-librarian
style—dark textured hair, tasteful glasses or a subtle piercing, and black, rust, or deep green
layers—inside a contemporary home library with books, wood, plants, and warm light. Avoid costume,
pin-up styling, stern academia, and exaggerated goth cues.

## Critic

An expressive raven with natural anatomy, sharp intelligence, skeptical side-eye, dry humor, and a
little sass. Use subtle posture and facial expression rather than human teeth or cartoon
exaggeration. Add a restrained brass collar tag and place the raven in a warm study with blurred
books, dark wood, paper, and amber light. Keep it cozy and sophisticated rather than gothic,
ominous, or mascot-like.

## Maker thinking state

Edit the settled Maker portrait while locking identity, age, hair, beard, clothing, workshop
setting, camera position, crop, lighting, color grade, and editorial realism. Change only his
natural expression and a few degrees of posture: glance subtly toward an imagined screen, lift one
eyebrow slightly, and relax his mouth into a thoughtful half-expression. Keep him casual, capable,
approachable, and untheatrical. No new objects, hands, text, logo, border, or watermark.

## Librarian thinking state

Edit the settled Librarian portrait while locking identity, age, hair and copper streaks, glasses,
piercings, clothing, library setting, camera position, crop, lighting, color grade, and editorial
realism. Change only her natural expression and a few degrees of posture: shift her gaze toward an
imagined item, lift one eyebrow with intelligent curiosity, and soften her smile into a knowing,
thoughtful half-expression. Keep her warm, grounded, approachable, and untheatrical. No new
objects, hands, text, logo, border, or watermark.
