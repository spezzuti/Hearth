# Milestone 55 — Living Room environmental proof

Hearth 0.57.0 moves beyond applying home-like materials to familiar app panels. Living Room is the
first room whose composition itself carries the metaphor while remaining a direct working surface.

## Environmental composition

- A deep painted arrival wall holds the room title, discussion search, and open conversations.
- Framed art and warm lamp light are joined by a realistic trailing houseplant that crosses the
  upper room boundary without becoming interactive scenery.
- The shared transcript is the dominant gathering surface, with a dark wood header, warm textile
  conversation layers, a faint fireplace/interior impression, and a grounded composer.
- The household rail is now an inhabited resident nook rather than a generic settings column.
- Resident portraits have equal weight at full size and remain recognizable in the condensed
  compact layout.

## Boundaries

- Conversation modes, transcripts, project context, private-history boundaries, handoffs, and
  persistence are unchanged.
- Compact layouts remove ornamental wall details before reducing type or hiding resident identity.
- The room does not require spatial navigation; every cue remains ordinary responsive interface.

## Verification

- Renderer build and TypeScript checks pass.
- The Living Room Electron continuity flow passes at full and compact desktop sizes.
- Visual QA caught and corrected compact user-message collapse and hidden resident portraits before
  release.
