# Milestone 54 — furnished shared and quiet rooms

Hearth 0.56.0 furnishes Living Room and Archive as complementary places for collective thought and
durable memory.

## Living Room

- The warmest shared palette in the house, with soft amber light, muted green structure, and a
  restrained communal floor treatment at full desktop size.
- Conversation, discussion tabs, composer, outcomes, and household context now use distinct but
  related materials so the room reads as one inhabited place.
- Resident cards preserve equal visual weight, larger recognizable portraits, and clear called-in
  state without turning the household rail into a settings panel.
- One-resident conversation, roundtables, pressure tests, private-history boundaries, and Workshop
  handoffs are unchanged.

## Archive

- Quiet plaster, cabinetry, labeled paper records, and stronger selected-record continuity between
  the list and detail pane.
- Provenance and project context are visually distinct from record content while staying calm and
  readable.
- Permanent removal is contained in a dedicated confirmation surface with an explicit irreversible
  boundary; no project-file semantics changed.
- Search, filtering, Return Packs, restoration, orientation, copy, and recovery behavior are
  unchanged.

## Verification

- Living Room remains horizontally and vertically contained at 1080×720.
- Archive remains horizontally and vertically contained at 1080×720.
- The pass caught and corrected two decoration-induced overflow regressions before release.
- Targeted Living Room and Archive Electron workflows pass before the complete release suite.
