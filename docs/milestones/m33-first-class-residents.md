# Milestone 33: First-class residents

Hearth 0.28.0 gives Maker and Librarian the visual weight and restrained expressiveness their roles
in the home deserve.

## Delivered

- Larger resident introductions on Home.
- A prominent Maker portrait in the Study conversation heading.
- Larger Maker presence in Workshop, Studio, and the Study status surface.
- Larger Librarian presence at the Library desk.
- Identity-preserved thinking portraits for Maker and Librarian.
- Smooth preloaded transitions between present and thinking states.
- Quiet living crops and truthful presence lights without mascot-style motion.
- Reduced-motion support inherited from Hearth's global accessibility rule.
- Electron coverage proving three residents and both expression assets load successfully.

## Tone

The residents do not wave, roam, emote at random, or interrupt the work. Their portraits settle
quietly when available and shift only while the actual person is considering a response.
