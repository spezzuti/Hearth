# Milestone 52 — furnished working rooms

Hearth 0.54.0 furnishes Study and Workshop as related but distinct places to think and execute.

## Study

- Warm editorial light, plaster, paper, linen, and restrained writing-desk accents.
- Clearer material separation for objectives, evidence, resident conversation, Critic review, and
  the project browser.
- Existing view tabs, handoffs, resident histories, objective editing, and review workflows remain
  unchanged.

## Workshop

- A smoky charcoal and green workroom surround with copper warmth and subtle technical structure.
- Stronger integration among the workbench, session shelf, controls, and Maker's warmer resident
  surface.
- The Claude Code workstream retains its established typography, terminal palette, information
  density, streaming behavior, modes, effort controls, diffs, and interruption semantics.
- Compact session controls were tightened and a new assertion proves the Context meter does not
  overlap Planning, effort, or Provider Doctor.

## Verification

- Study and managed Workshop workflows pass at full desktop size.
- Workshop document, layout, workbench, Maker rail, composer, context inspector, and session controls
  remain contained at 1100×720.
- TypeScript and production renderer builds pass before the complete release suite.
