# Milestone 32: Expressive Companion

Hearth 0.27.0 replaces Companion's prototype shell with its finished code-native character system.

## Delivered

- A warm hearthling silhouette with an expressive face, ember, small arms, and grounded posture.
- Quiet idle breathing and occasional blinking.
- Attentive expression while Companion conversation is open.
- A distinct thinking state while a reply is in progress.
- A brief lit reply acknowledgement for responses arriving while the popover is closed.
- One shared character anatomy in the floating control and conversation heading.
- Visible keyboard focus and global reduced-motion support.
- Electron screenshots and regression assertions for idle and open character states.

## Restraint

Companion remains ambient. It does not roam across the interface, cover the terminal, perform canned
observations, or demand acknowledgement. Its expression reflects real conversation state and then
settles back into the room.
