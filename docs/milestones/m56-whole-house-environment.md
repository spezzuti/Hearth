# Milestone 56 — whole-house environmental pass

Hearth 0.57.0 now carries the Living Room's environmental-interface standard through every room.
The result is a coherent home made of direct working surfaces, not a virtual building.

## Room outcomes

- Home uses a warm morning wall, grounded return surface, and calm household materials.
- Study reads as an inhabited office around briefs, project review, Critic, and Maker.
- Workshop surrounds the unchanged terminal and managed Maker flow with a detailed workbench.
- Library combines continuous shelves, reading light, book-like reference cards, and a raised
  Librarian desk.
- Studio uses a bright creative-loft wall and tactile worktable without weakening idea capture.
- Living Room uses a generated, photorealistic olive-and-walnut interior behind the central
  gathering surface, with seating, textiles, books, and fireplace light visible around the working
  panels. The existing shelf, lamp, and trailing plant remain separate foreground elements, and
  equal resident identity is preserved at compact size.
- Archive fades from illuminated shelves and records into a quiet recovery surface with explicit
  destructive boundaries.

## House continuity

- Persistent toolbar materials are warm and restrained across all rooms.
- Each room remains visually distinct without changing navigation, terminology, or workflow.
- Decorative imagery stays noninteractive and below accessible content.
- Compact layouts preserve readable type, controls, resident identity, and terminal space before
  ornament.

## Verification

- The production renderer and desktop build pass.
- Living Room conversation, project continuity, Studio lifecycle, Study and project review,
  Library, Archive recovery, and Workshop ConPTY flows pass in Electron.
- Wide and compact screenshots were inspected for clipping, overflow, toolbar balance, background
