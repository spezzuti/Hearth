# Milestone 53 — furnished creative rooms

Hearth 0.55.0 furnishes Library and Studio as connected places for keeping useful material and
letting ideas develop.

## Library

- Warmer reading light, shelf depth, paper texture, and green reference materials.
- More tactile catalog controls and saved-item cards without changing search, sorting, collections,
  discovery, import, or filing behavior.
- A larger Librarian portrait and stronger desk surface at full and compact desktop sizes.
- Resident messages remain readable, naturally wrapping, immediately posted, and anchored to the
  latest conversation.

## Studio

- A brighter contemporary-loft palette with amber light and muted green structure.
- Drafting-grid work surfaces, ruled capture paper, status-colored counters, and restrained tape
  details on idea cards.
- The same material identity extends to Notes without merging ideas and notes or changing their
  canonical destinations.
- Existing rest, pursue, let-go, Maker discussion, project connection, search, edit, and archive
  workflows remain unchanged.

## Verification

- Library remains horizontally and vertically contained at 1080×720.
- Studio remains horizontally contained at 1040×700 with readable cards and controls.
- TypeScript and targeted Library and Studio Electron workflows pass before the complete release
  suite.
